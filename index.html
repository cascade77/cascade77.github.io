I am a rising final-year undergraduate studying bioinformatics at NUST. My interests are in formal verification, particularly interactive theorem proving and its application to systems software. I work mainly with the Rocq theorem prover, using it for C verification of kernel code and for reasoning about programming-language semantics.

Here is my CV. I am happy to talk about theorem proving, systems verification, programming-language verification, or related work. You can reach me by email.
